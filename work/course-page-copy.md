# Expanded FAQ

Keep the seven existing answers where noted; the rest are new.

### About the program

**Who is this program for?**
Adults who function well in most of their life and struggle specifically
with saying no, holding limits, and stopping other people's problems from
becoming their own. Most clients are professionals, caregivers, or the
"reliable one" in their family.

**Is this therapy?**
No. This is coaching and education, not psychotherapy, and it is not a
substitute for mental health treatment. Dana is a licensed psychotherapist
and brings 15+ years of clinical training to how she teaches — but in this
program she is working as a coach. If something surfaces that calls for
therapy, she'll tell you and help you think about next steps.
*[Dana to confirm wording.]*

**Do I need to live in Tennessee?**
No. Dana's therapy practice is limited to Tennessee residents, but
coaching is not therapy and is not geographically restricted, so you can
join from anywhere. Calls are on Zoom.
*[Dana to confirm wording.]*

**I've already read the boundaries books. How is this different?**
The books gave you the concept. This gives you the application. The
difference is the private calls — where you bring the actual relationship,
the actual conversation you're dreading, and work out what to say and how
to hold it when the other person pushes back.

**How much time does this take each week?**
Around 30–45 minutes for the videos and exercises, plus your scheduled
calls. The modules are short by design.

**What if I fall behind?**
You have lifetime access to the course, and calls are booked on your
availability, so you set the pace. Life happening is not a reason to drop
out — it's usually the material showing up in real time.

**Is this a Christian program?**
No. One module — *"Am I Being Hateful?" Setting Boundaries as a Christian*
— addresses the specific guilt many Christians feel about saying no,
because clients kept asking for it. The other thirteen modules are secular
and you can skip that one entirely.

**Will this fix the difficult person in my life?**
No program can change someone who isn't in it. What changes is what you
do — what you accept, what you say, what you stop absorbing. That shift
usually changes the dynamic, and sometimes it changes the relationship. It
does not require the other person's cooperation.

### The coaching

**How do the coaching calls work?** *(existing — update)*
After you enrol you'll get access to Dana's scheduling calendar via
Calendly. Each call is 90 minutes and tailored to applying the course
content to your specific situation.

**Is the $250 intro session included?** *(new)*
Yes. Your first call is the 90-minute deep dive into your history and
current dynamics, and it's included in every package — you don't pay the
intro fee on top.

**What is a Boundary Booster?** *(existing — keep)*
A scheduled 15-minute Zoom call. You complete a short form beforehand to
identify the specific issue, so the call gets straight to it. They're for
the moment right before or right after a hard conversation. If you need
more depth, you can book a full session. All Boosters must be scheduled
within 60 days of enrolment.

**Can I upgrade to a bigger package later?**
Yes. If you start with one package and want more coaching, the difference
can be applied — talk to Dana and she'll sort it out.

**What happens when my calls are used up?**
You keep lifetime access to the course, and you can book further sessions
at Dana's standard rate whenever you want them. Many clients come back for
a single session when a new situation arises.

### Packages and payment

**What's the difference between the packages?** *(existing — update)*
All three include the full course, Boundary Boosters, and lifetime access.
The difference is the number of private 90-minute calls: four, six, or
eight. Unity is built for couples working through this together.

**Which package should I choose?**
Four calls if you have one or two specific relationships to work on. Six
if the pattern runs through most of your life and you want room to unpack
where it came from. Unity if you and your partner want to do this
together.

**Do you offer payment plans?**
Yes — all three packages can be paid in three monthly instalments.

**I already bought the $99 self-paced course. Can I apply it?**
Yes. The full $99 is credited toward any package if you upgrade within 90
days of buying the course.

**Can I do the course without the coaching calls?** *(existing — fix link)*
Yes — the self-paced Boundary Blueprint is available on its own for $99 at
[danaskaggs.thinkific.com](https://danaskaggs.thinkific.com/).
Clients see the strongest results when the course is paired with
coaching, but the course stands on its own.

**What's your refund policy?**
There's a 100% money-back guarantee for 7 days after purchase, provided
you haven't accessed the course content and the program hasn't started.
After that — or once you've opened the materials or had your first
session — refunds aren't available. Full terms are on the
[Refund Policy](https://danaskaggs.com/refund-policy) page.

**Can you guarantee results?**
No, and be wary of anyone who does. What's guaranteed is the structure,
the material, and Dana's attention. The conversations are yours to have.
