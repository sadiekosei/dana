<p>Picture a really awful day, the kind where, and I'm speaking from personal experience here, I could eat my way down through a mountain of donuts and call it coping. If you've ever punished yourself with food, treated it like your enemy, your life preserver, or your buffer against pain, you already know how tangled your relationship with food can get. Untangling exactly that is what nutrition coach Shelley Loving does for a living.</p>

<p>Shelley's husband had a heart attack in 2014, and three years later that scare sent her looking for a more holistic way to feed her family. She trained as an Integrative Nutrition Health Coach at the Institute for Integrative Nutrition, then became a certified culinary nutrition expert through the Academy for Culinary Nutrition in 2019, and a certified professional cook from the Ruby Online Culinary School in the summer of 2021. Along the way she realized that while she was cooking to heal her husband, she was healing herself too.</p>

<p>We get into why counting calories and carbs never worked for either of us, her 80-20 rule for enjoying the foods you love without guilt, how to read an ingredient list instead of a nutrition facts panel, and the three baby steps she hands anyone who feels too overwhelmed to start. Grace makes an appearance too, because as Shelley puts it, you're allowed to eat the donut.</p>
