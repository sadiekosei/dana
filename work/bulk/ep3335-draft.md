Nico Lagan grew up without a present father figure, idolized the wrong men, and spent his late teens and early twenties as an addict and a drug dealer before that one phone call sent him back to his mother's house, into NA and AA meetings, and eventually into the career he has now.</p>

We talk about what actually changes a person's life (his answer: pain, not comfort), why he cut ties with nearly everyone around him to get sober, and the five virtues he now teaches men: courage, being a protector, being a provider, temperance, and faith.</p>

<p>Nico's content gets called controversial, and in this conversation he explains why he doesn't see it that way, and makes his case for the differences he believes men and women bring to a relationship. Whether or not you agree with everything he says, his own story, from an eviction notice to accountability, is worth hearing.</p>
