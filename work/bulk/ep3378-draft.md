<p>We get into what relational trauma actually looks like when your childhood home had food, a lawn, and parents who loved you, why "don't talk, don't trust, don't feel" are the three rules that keep dysfunctional families running, and how to hold a boundary calmly instead of waiting until you explode.</p>
