<p><em>First aired February 9, 2025</em></p>

<p>Marni Battista was skiing a double black diamond in new boots, feeling badass, when she went flying through the air and landed on her sacrum. Seven fractures. Twelve weeks flat on her back, unable to work, read, knit, or even sit up, using an old-lady reaching claw because she couldn't move without pain. The doctors kept saying she should be better by now. She wasn't. Finally a guide told her something that stopped her cold: you broke your foundation because you built your whole life on doing and producing, and disconnected the emotional part of yourself. It was time to rebuild.</p>

<p>Marni is an entrepreneur, author, and transformational coach whose new book, <em>Your Radical Living Challenge: Seven Questions for a Meaningful Life</em>, comes out February 5. Before the injury, she'd built a nearly $2-million-a-year dating coaching business, appeared in the LA Times and the New Yorker, and been on Dr. Phil, Ryan Seacrest, and Home & Family TV, all while quietly wondering how much longer she could keep pushing. We talk about how to live life on your own terms instead of the version everyone else expects of you, why "comparison despair" wrecks so many of us (especially after 18 months off social media forced her to notice it), and how she finally answered her own website's question: if you could create your whole life any way you wanted it, what would it look like?</p>

<p>We also get into the harder, practical side of reinvention: how women specifically build the financial clarity to back up a courageous decision, and why the "essential problem" is almost never the problem you think it is.</p>
